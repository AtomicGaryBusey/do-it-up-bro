# Stop conditions

Converge when acceptance criteria pass, consequential claims have evidence, relevant objective checks pass, material reviewer objections are resolved or explicitly accepted by the user, and another exploration wave is unlikely to alter the result.

Track what changed after each wave. Continue when a material contradiction remains and an affordable test or independent perspective can resolve it. Stop repetitive exploration that produces neither new evidence nor changed decisions. User time, budget, and pause instructions remain binding even when work is incomplete.

On a blocker, report completed work, the exact unmet criterion, evidence of the blocker, and the smallest next action needed. Do not label partial success complete. On success, provide the deliverable and checks, with residual uncertainty and failed alternatives only where they affect use of the result.
